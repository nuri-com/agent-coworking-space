THE SMALL PRINT, IN HUMAN.

Good questions.

So, what is this?+

A physical coworking concept with free AI credits included in the day pass. This is the public launch preview. Venue openings, sponsor agreements and supported models are still being confirmed.

What's included for €29 / $29?+

The proposed pass covers your desk, coffee, Wi-Fi and personal on-site AI API access, from 10am to 8pm local time. Payment, tax and model-access terms will be finalized before bookings open.

Is the AI access unlimited?+

Unlimited on-site access is the intended offer, not an active or contracted service yet. The supported models, sponsor allocation and usage conditions must be confirmed and published before anyone books.

Can I book online or just walk in?+

That's the plan: book on demand, or walk in if a desk is available. A full space means no walk-in access. For now, the website prepares an enquiry only. No availability is simulated, no seat is reserved and no payment is taken.

Do sponsors get my work or contact details?+

No automatic contact exports or session sharing. You can separately request introductions or information about research. Research interest is not permission to train on your work; that would need a specific, informed agreement. Future model-provider processing terms must be disclosed before AI access launches.
