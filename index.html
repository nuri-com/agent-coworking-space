THE FINE PRINT, IN BIG TYPE

Good questions.

Is the day pass the same as the accelerator?+

No. The day pass gives you a desk, Wi-Fi, coffee and the daily AI hour (2–3 pm, unlimited in that hour). The accelerator is a separate program for startups and studios with its own terms. Requesting a desk signs nothing.

Is the accelerator's AI free?+

No upfront AI bill. Credits you actually consume convert into an uncapped SAFE, which may become shares in a future financing under its terms. The 150k ceiling is a maximum allocation, not unlimited spend. Denomination, valuation and the issuing company are agreed in your offer before you sign.

Can we use any model?+

Any model available through our router, within your approved allocation.

Can I book a desk now?+

Send your city and date. We confirm venue, availability and the final offer before booking. Today this site prepares an email request: no desk is reserved, no payment taken, no key issued.

What does a pass cost?+

Proposed: €29 Berlin, TZS 75,000 Arusha and Zanzibar, AED 105 Dubai, TRY 1,400 Istanbul, $29 Palo Alto. 10am–8pm locally, AI hour 2–3 pm included.

Do partners get my stuff?+

No. No auto-sharing of contacts, prompts, code or sessions. Introductions happen only after separate approval, research and training only with a separate agreement. Forms prepare emails on your device; nothing is sent until you send it.
