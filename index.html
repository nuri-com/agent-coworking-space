04 / THE PRACTICAL THINGS

A few good
questions.

An honest look at what's planned,
and what's still being worked out.

Can I book a desk today?+

Not yet. This is a launch preview, not an operating booking service. You can prepare an enquiry for Berlin, Arusha or Dubai. No seat is reserved, no payment is taken and nothing is sent until you send the email yourself.

What's included in the proposed day pass?+

The proposal is €29 or $29 per person for a day of workspace, coffee, Wi-Fi and on-site AI API access, from 10am to 8pm local time. Venue details, payment and tax terms, model access and usage terms must be confirmed before bookings open.

Is the AI access really unlimited?+

Unlimited AI is an intended launch offer, not a service currently delivered. Model allocations are not yet contracted. The model lineup, budgets, fair-use conditions and other usage limits will be published before anyone can book.

Can I walk in, or check live availability?+

Real-time capacity is not available in this preview. Once venues are operating, walk-ins would only be possible if there is availability. No current desk availability, venue address or opening date is confirmed.

Who can see my work? Is it used for training?+

Your prompts, code and API sessions are not shared. Research participation would require separate informed consent. An optional request for research information is not consent to training. Any future provider processing and retention terms must be disclosed before access launches.

This preview prepares an email locally; it does not automatically submit your form data. You choose whether to send the draft through your own email application.

Will sponsors get my contact details?+

Only if you voluntarily request introductions. The optional introduction checkbox is separate from the request for research information. Neither gives sponsors access to your prompts, code or API sessions. There are no automatic contact exports or blanket training rights.
