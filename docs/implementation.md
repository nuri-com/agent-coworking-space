# Landing preview implementation

## Why

Make the physical offer understandable before building a live booking or inference service. Keep the sponsor pitch ambitious without inventing operational venues or confirmed endorsements.

## Acceptance

1. Original responsive landing page; verified at 3840 x 2160 and mobile, with no horizontal overflow.
2. Clickable location/date/currency enquiry flow, valid dates per city timezone, EUR 29/USD 29 and 10:00-20:00 local hours. No fabricated booking or API-key success.
3. Separate sponsor enquiry. Contact introductions optional and unchecked; research interest is not consent to training. No visitor-data storage, trackers, automatic sends or training collection.
4. Clear preview, conceptual imagery and pending partner labels; all published claims consistent with README.
5. Node logic tests, browser interaction tests, screenshots, reviewed public draft PR. No domain registration, DNS or production deployment.

## Delivery boundary

The UI prepares email drafts. Real capacity, payments, check-in, key issuance/revocation and separately documented research consent remain future server-side work. Nuri app and website worktrees stay untouched.
